# Source quality, from strongest to weakest

1. The primary document: the standard, the specification, the law, the paper, the changelog.
2. The maintainer's or vendor's own documentation and announcements.
3. A well-known reference with editorial review.
4. A recent, detailed article by a named author, with its own sources.
5. Forum answers and comments: useful for leads, weak as evidence.
6. Content with no date, no author and no sources: a lead at best.

Always prefer the page that would be cited by the others.
